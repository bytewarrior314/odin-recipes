Brownies

Description

This is a recipe for moist and fudgy brownies.

The recipe takes at most 20 minutes!

Ingredients

  • Sugar: 2 cups
  • Flour: 1 1/4 cups
  • Melted unsalted butter: 1 cup
  • Eggs: 4 large
  • Cocoa powder: 1/2 cup
  • Walnuts: 1/2 cup
  • Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
  • Baking powder: 1/2 tsp
  • Salt: 1/2 tsp
