Cornbread

Description

This recipe is for a moist and crumbly cornbread.

Cornbread is one favorite southern style dishes.

Ingredients

  • Flour: 2 cups
  • Cornmeal: 1 cup
  • Sugar: 1 cup
  • Baking powder: 1 1/2 tbsp
  • Salt: 1 tsp
  • Melted butter: 1/2 cup
  • Oil: 1/2 cup
  • Milk: 1 1/4 cup
  • Eggs: 3 large
  • Honey and butter for serving
