Rice and Beans

Ingredients

  • Olive oil: 2 tbsp
  • Diced onion: 1 small
  • Diced poblano pepper: 1 small
  • Chopped garlic: 1 clove
  • Basmati rice: 1 cup
  • Paprika: 2 tsp
  • Ground cummin: 1 tsp
  • Dried oregano: 1 tsp
  • Tomato paste: 2 tbsp
  • Vegetable broth: 2 cups
  • Canned Black beans: 1 can
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in medium pot over medium-high heat
  2. Saute onion, poblano pepper, and garlic for 2 to 3 minutes
  3. Add rice and cook for about 2 minutes; stir occasionally
  4. Season with paprika, cumin, oregano, salt and black pepper
  5. Stir in tomato paste and cook for 1 to 2 minutes
  6. Add vegetable broth and black beans
  7. Bring to a boil and then reduce the heat to low
  8. Cover and simmer for about 15 to 20 minutes until the rice is tender
  9. Remove the pot from the heat and keep it covered
  10. Let it sit for about 5 minutes then fluff the rice with a fork
  11. EAT! Don't burn your mouth!
